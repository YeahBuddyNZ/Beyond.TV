Beyond™ TV

Free your world. One calm home screen for the whole whānau.

Download Beyond™ TV
  1. Press OK on the button. When it's downloaded, choose Install, then Open.
    If the TV asks, allow the Downloader app to install apps.
  2. To make it your home screen, turn on debugging once: Settings → System → About, press Build seven times, then Settings → System → Developer options → USB debugging on.
  3. In Beyond™ TV, choose Home Screen → Beyond™ TV, and tick Always allow when the TV asks.

Your promo apps are switched off, never uninstalled, and Home Screen → Original brings it all back. Beyond™ TV keeps itself up to date: when there's a new version, the TV asks, and one OK installs it.
