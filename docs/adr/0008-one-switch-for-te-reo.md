# 0008: One switch for te reo

Accepted, 23/10/2026.

## Decision

*Te reo Māori* in Home Settings (it was *Te reo word of the day*) turns all the te reo on the TV on or off together: the word of the day on the home screen and screensaver, and the greeting. With it off, "Kia ora, Mum" becomes "Hello, Mum", "Kia ora, whānau" becomes "Hello, everyone", and the same goes for the welcome when somebody signs in. It stays on unless they turn it off, and it keeps the same saved setting (`kupu`), so a TV that had the word of the day off now has te reo off.

## Why

Kyle asked to be able to turn te reo off too. Te reo is part of how Beyond TV sounds in Aotearoa, so it is on by default; some homes would still rather it wasn't on their screen, and that's their call. One switch is easier to find and explain than one per place.

## What it rules out

Te reo turning up somewhere new without following this switch. Names of things that are simply their names (whānau in a show's description, the app's own content) are not covered.
