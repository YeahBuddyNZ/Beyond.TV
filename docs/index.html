Beyond™ TV

Free your world.

One calm home screen for the whole whānau. Beyond™ TV swaps your TV's ad-filled home screen for a quiet one: your streaming apps on one shelf, what's worth watching tonight, and nothing trying to sell you anything. Free, and built in Aotearoa by Beyond.

What it does now

No ads, no clutter

A clean home screen in place of the sponsored rows, with a one-tap way back to the original.

Your apps, one shelf

Netflix, Neon, TVNZ+, Disney+, ThreeNow, Plex and the rest, as square icons with their names underneath, like an iPad, in the order you like.

What's on tonight

Trending films and shows, picked only from the services you already have.

Every big TV brand

Google TV and Android TV, LG and Samsung, all with the same home screen.

NewA screen that breathes

Beyond purple, day and night: soft violet light that rises and falls six times a minute, slowing down as the evening does, with tiny sparkles drifting up through it. Calm, never still.

NewTalk to Beyond

Press the four dots, say what would make it better, and we read every one. If we reply, it shows up on your TV.

NewFamily, all together

"Who's watching?" now starts with Family: everyone on the couch at once, with picks the whole family can watch. Then a profile for each person, and kids' profiles with their own apps behind a grown-up PIN.

NewTwo homes, one show

For families across two homes. Start it from the Beyond Family app and every TV counts down together, so you all press play at the same moment. Reactions fly between phones and onto the TV. On LG and Samsung now, Google TV soon.

NewEarn your screen

Switch it on in Beyond Family and a kid's apps wait until today's jobs are ticked off. The TV shows what's left, and opens up the moment the last one is done. On LG and Samsung now, Google TV soon.

NewThe house noticeboard

When nobody's watching, the TV shows today beside the clock: who's where, what's for tea, birthdays, and the notes you left each other in Beyond Family. Switch it on in the app. On LG and Samsung now, Google TV soon.

NewSleep timer

Falling asleep in front of a show? Sleep Timer in the menu turns the TV off after 15, 30, 60 or 90 minutes, with a heads-up a minute before.

NewBedtime breathing

A soft violet night light that breathes with the kids as they wind down, slower and slower, then fades to dark. Start it from the moon on the home screen in the evening, or from your phone. On LG and Samsung now, Google TV soon.

NewHave the pets been fed?

When the kids turn the TV on, it asks whether the cat (or the dog) got fed today. "Yes" ticks it off in Beyond Family, so everyone knows. On LG and Samsung now, Google TV soon.

NewFine day nudge

When it's mild and sunny, the TV suggests going outside for a bit. When you're back, it counts the time as green time in Beyond Family. Once a day at most. On LG and Samsung now, Google TV soon.

NewDinner question

Put one question on the TV from Beyond Family. Everyone answers from their phone, faces fill in as they do, and the answers come up together so nobody copies. On LG and Samsung now, Google TV soon.

NewTe reo word of the day

A new kupu on the home screen every day, with what it means, and on the screensaver how to say it. Everyday words, so it turns up at tea. On Google TV, LG and Samsung.

Put it on your TV

For Google TV and Android TV (Android 8 or newer). About five minutes, and nothing is rooted or uninstalled. First, on the TV: Settings → System → About, press Build seven times, then Settings → System → Developer options → turn on USB debugging.

No computerStraight on the TV
  1. Install Downloader from the Play Store on the TV.
  2. Type yeahbuddynz.github.io/Beyond.TV/tv and press Go.
  3. Press Download, then Install and Open.
Mac or LinuxOne line in Terminal 
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/YeahBuddyNZ/Beyond.TV/main/install.sh | bash

It finds your TV on the Wi-Fi, installs it and tells you what to press.

WindowsOne line in PowerShell 
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/YeahBuddyNZ/Beyond.TV/main/install.ps1 | iex

The same, for Windows. Nothing to install first.

Then, in Beyond™ TV on the TV: Home Screen → Beyond™ TV. Home Screen → Original brings the old one back any time. LG and Samsung TVs: see the guide.

What it tells us. Once a day the TV sends Beyond a made-up id, its city (to about 10km), its make, model and versions, and its own error messages, so we can fix a problem on your kind of TV before you have to ask. Never what you watch, never your name. Home Settings → Help fix problems turns it off and deletes what we had.
